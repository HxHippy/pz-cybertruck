description=[h1]Stainless. Armored. Silent.[/h1]
description=A fully loaded electric Cybertruck for Build 42. There's no gas tank, so you charge it off a generator.
description=
description=[h2]New in 1.5[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Apocalypse fittings you can see: saw blades on the rockers and bumpers, a roof hatch, and a roof gun that swings to face what it's shooting.
description=[*]A grinder whir for the blades and a proper bite when they hit something.
description=[*]Elon's Musk in every glovebox.
description=[*]Steadier on the road, and the log spam that caused lag is cut right down.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Electric drive[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]A 100% battery pack replaces the gas tank. The dash gauge shows your charge.
description=[*]It draws almost nothing while idling and drains faster the harder you drive. Slowing down puts a little charge back.
description=[*]Gas cans and pumps don't work on it.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Charging[/h2]
description=Park within 12 tiles of a running generator, right-click the truck, and pick [b]Plug in to charge[/b]. Charging burns generator fuel. While the grid is still up, you can charge next to a powered building for free.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HxHippy/pz-cybertruck/main/docs/cybertruck-plug.png[/img]
description=
description=[h2]All the works[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Near-silent motor.[/b] Engine loudness is 20 (the vanilla SUV is 100), and it runs on a quiet electric whine instead of a V8.
description=[*][b]Armored glass and stainless panels.[/b] Against weapons the glass is 30x tougher than vanilla and the panels are 12x tougher. Both soak up 90% of zombie hits and crash damage.
description=[*][b]Top-tier parts.[/b] Modern tires, brakes and suspension, a HAM radio, 4 seats, and a 160-unit locking vault.
description=[*][b]Plush cabin.[/b] Crashes and zombie impacts can't hurt anyone inside.
description=[*][b]Built for the rough stuff.[/b] Brush and grass barely slow it down.
description=[*][b]Tough chassis.[/b] Front and rear crash health of 600 and a 120 km/h top speed.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HxHippy/pz-cybertruck/main/docs/cybertruck-parts.png[/img]
description=
description=[h2]Getting one[/h2]
description=Both options are sandbox toggles:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]World spawn:[/b] rare, in upscale neighborhoods, professional lots and luxury dealerships. The weight is adjustable.
description=[*][b]Build it:[/b] Build Cybertruck (Mechanics 8, Welding 7, Electrical 6). It rolls out next to you with the key and a nearly empty battery.
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Apocalypse fittings[/h2]
description=The truck you find is still just the truck. The rest is bolted on, and every part of it has a dial.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Blade kit.[/b] Saws along the rockers, behind the rear wheels and across the front bumper. The driver spins them up from the V menu, and they run on the pack. They shred any zombie that touches the truck, stop bodies from costing you speed, and fell ordinary trees in your path for the wood. Giant trees stay standing. Both are sandbox dials if you'd rather turn them off.
description=[*][b]Roof hatch and gun.[/b] Point with the mouse and hold to fire, or hold K. The target lights up red and the gun swings to face it. The front passenger can shoot on the move, and the driver can shoot once the truck stops. It eats loose 5.56 from your pockets or the vault, and it's loud.
description=[*][b]Bumper charging.[/b] Off unless you turn it on. A zombie the bumper actually kills puts a set percent back in the pack.
description=[/list]
description=Craft the parts under Metalworking, then install them from the mechanics screen. Found trucks come bare unless you turn that dial on. A truck you build always comes bare.
description=

description=[h2]Elon's Musk[/h2]
description=Every Cybertruck has a bottle in the glovebox. Right-click it and pick [b]Spritz on some of Elon's Musk[/b]. For the next in-game hour, any zombie within 10 tiles drops you and walks the other way. Five spritzes to a bottle, and they stack.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HxHippy/pz-cybertruck/main/docs/cybertruck-musk.png[/img]
description=
description=Nearly everything is a sandbox dial: spawn weight, battery when found, drain, regen, charge time, generator fuel cost, cable reach, motor power, top speed, loudness, off-road grip, glass and panel strength, armor, cabin protection (plush tank, reinforced or vanilla), and the fittings above.
description=Find them in New Game > Sandbox > Advanced > Cybertruck. Playing solo? The same dials are in Options > Mods > Cybertruck. Tick [b]Use these settings in single player[/b] and they take effect instantly.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=Charging, drain, armor, the blades and every roof-gun shot run on the server. Plugging in is checked by the server. Elon's Musk is used up on the server, and each player's game steers the zombies it turns away.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42 only.
description=[*]Safe to add to an existing save. New trucks spawn in chunks you haven't explored yet.
